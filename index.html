Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for live sound, recording and streaming rigs where every millisecond and every channel counts.

Kernel-streaming engine

Talks to the audio driver directly (WDM-KS), so devices that are disabled in the Windows Sound panel can still be used as ASIO devices.

Per-channel matrix

Switch every single input and output channel on or off. Expose only channel 2 of a stereo mic, or just the left speaker.

Auto Buffer

Learns the smallest stable buffer: it lowers after clean sessions and raises it after dropouts, within limits you set.

WASAPI exclusive & shared

Event-driven streaming with MMCSS "Pro Audio" thread priority, small-period shared mode and per-device Auto / Exclusive / Shared choice.

Multi-device aggregation

Combine several sound cards into one ASIO device with drift correction, per-device gain and extra delay.

Live monitor

Channel meters, xrun counters, callback timing and latency estimates while your DAW is streaming.

Channel matrix

Choose exactly which channels your DAW sees

Every enabled device shows a row of numbered buttons. Lit means the channel appears in your DAW, dark means it's hidden. Channels keep their real numbers.

  • Inputs and outputs. Works for WASAPI and kernel-streaming devices.
  • ALL / NONE flips a whole device in one click.
  • Remembered. Choices are saved per device and survive unplugging.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can it use a device that is disabled in Windows?

Yes. Devices shown with a [KS] tag are opened directly through the driver's kernel-streaming interface, so the Windows "Disabled" setting doesn't apply to them. Results depend on the sound-card driver, and a device that Windows is actively using may refuse to open.

Is there really zero latency?

No ASIO driver has zero latency. Latency is set by the buffer size, the device's own minimum period and the hardware driver. AMO ASIO gives you full control over buffers, safety offsets and the Auto Buffer learner so you can get as low as your hardware allows.

Which programs does it work with?

Any ASIO host: DAWs, live-sound software, OBS-style audio tools and measurement apps. Both 64-bit and 32-bit hosts are supported.

How do I uninstall it?

Use Settings → Apps (Add/Remove Programs) or the "Uninstall AMO ASIO" shortcut in the Start menu. Your settings are kept so a reinstall remembers them.

Windows says "unknown publisher" when I run the installer.

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info → Run anyway if you downloaded it from this page.
